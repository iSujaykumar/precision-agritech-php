STAGING CHECKS — UPI QR AND ADMIN

1. Import database/upgrade.sql in phpMyAdmin. Import it again. Both times should finish. If procedures are blocked, use Settings → Database update twice instead.
2. Open Payments. Save the real UPI ID and payee name. QR source stays on auto-generate.
3. The preview should show a square QR, the payee name and ₹1. Download the SVG and open it. It should be a picture, not an error.
4. Place a small UPI order on a phone. On the order page, scan the QR with a real UPI app. Check the payee, the amount and the order number before you pay. Pay ₹1 only if you are willing to refund it.
5. Enter the 12-character UTR. In the desk, mark that payment received. Stock should stay sold, not return to the shelf.
6. Change the UPI ID to "not valid" and save. The shop must not show a broken QR. Put the real ID back.
7. Switch QR source to the uploaded image, place another test order, and confirm the amount is still written in words. Switch back to auto-generate.
8. Untick UPI and save. Checkout must not offer UPI. Tick it again.
9. Create a staff login with role staff. That person must not open Payments or Staff. A manager must not open Staff. The owner still can.
10. Homepage: change the headline, save, look at the home page, then put the old headline back.
11. Offers: put one tray on an offer badge, confirm the shop shows Offer, then clear it.
12. Shop paused: turn it on, confirm checkout will not place an order, then turn it off.
13. On a 360px-wide phone, the order QR should fit and the Pay with UPI app button should be easy to tap.
14. Download the 7-day payments CSV. A customer name that starts with = must not become a formula.
15. Email and a real UPI app scan can only be confirmed by you on staging. This copy was not checked against a live bank.
