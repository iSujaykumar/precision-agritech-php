Precision Agritech responsive notes
13 Oct 2026

What was already working
The shop and the admin area already reflow. The viewport tag is width=device-width, initial-scale=1, viewport-fit=cover. There is no maximum-scale and pinch zoom is left on. Images scale, the page wrap is min(72rem, 100% minus the side gap), and wide tables sit in .table-wrap so they scroll inside the table, not the page. Product rails scroll sideways on their own. The hero is 70vh with 70svh after it. The calculator panel is 100vh with 100dvh after it, and on a phone it is a bottom sheet. The UPI mark is fluid, never under about 180px, with white padding and pixelated rendering. Buttons are at least 44px. The header, the bottom bar and the WhatsApp button already pad for the notch and the home indicator.

What was changed
assets/css/site.css
On screens up to 700px the sticky cart bar sits 8px above the fixed bottom bar. The page padding is tall enough that the last content is not hidden behind either bar, including the home-indicator inset. Text fields use at least 16px type on those screens so a phone does not zoom in when a field is focused. The admin finder goes full width there. Filter chips keep an 8px gap on a coarse pointer.

assets/css/admin.css
Admin fields under 700px use at least 16px type and a 44px tap height. Admin cards stay one column under 480px, two columns from there up to the tablet sizes, and three on a desktop. That rule was already in this file. It was checked again, not rewritten.

assets/js/calc.js
If the calculator sheet is open, the screen is under 701px, and the keyboard covers more than 40px, the sheet moves up by that amount. Otherwise the inline position is cleared and the CSS rules apply.

tests/homepage_checks.php
The check named "page does not scroll sideways" used to look for overflow-x: hidden on the whole page. That rule is not in the CSS, on purpose. Hiding overflow covers a real overflow instead of fixing it, and it can break sticky bars. The check now looks for overflow-wrap on the body and for .table-wrap scrolling on its own, and it fails if html, body is given overflow-x: hidden.

Left alone, and why
The old breakpoints (640, 700, 800, 900, 1024) were not replaced with a new set. The shop grid was not switched to auto-fill. Both would change how many cards a tablet shows. The current steps already give 1, 2, 3 and 4 columns at the widths above.
Admin tables were not rebuilt as cards. They already sit in .table-wrap with a sticky first column. A card layout would need a label on every cell and would be a second design.
Long paragraphs were not narrowed to 65 characters. The 72rem wrap is the desktop measure.
Phone numbers inside a footer sentence stay on the line. Turning them into 44px blocks would open up the footer spacing that was fixed earlier. The Shop and Company links are the tall tap targets.
Google sign-in is not on this copy, so there is no Google button to resize. The six code boxes appear only after a code is sent.
/farmer-prices returns 404 for a visitor who is not allowed to see farmer rates. That is access control, not a layout bug.
MySQL 5.7, MySQL 8, a live UPI payment and a live WhatsApp send were not run.
