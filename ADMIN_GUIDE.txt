ADMIN GUIDE — Precision Agritech

UPI QR
1. Open Nursery desk → Settings → Payments.
2. Enter the UPI ID (like name@bank) and the payee name customers should see.
3. Leave QR source on "Auto-generate from UPI ID".
4. Save. The preview is a sample for ₹1 with the note TEST. It is not a real charge.
5. Download SVG or PNG, or print it, if you want a copy for the counter.
6. After a customer places a UPI order, their order page shows a QR for that exact total and that order number.
7. They still send the 12-character UTR. You still mark the payment received yourself. The site does not check the bank.
8. If you already have a printed QR image, switch QR source to "Use uploaded image" and upload it. The amount will be written next to the picture, because a static image cannot change per order.

Turn a method off
On Payments, untick UPI, bank transfer or cash on delivery. At least one must stay on. Customers only see the methods you leave on.

Homepage
Settings → Homepage. The boxes start with the current wording. Change a headline or hide a rail, then save. Button links must start with / and stay on this site.

Offers
Products → Offers. Tick trays, optionally set start and end, then choose "Offer badge only" or "Set old price". The old price must be higher than the selling price. Clear offer removes the badge and the old price.

Pages
Settings → Pages. One paragraph per line. Save an empty box to go back to the original page.

Pause orders
Settings → Shop → Shop paused. Customers can still browse. Checkout will not take a new order, and a short note appears at the top of the shop.

Desk roles
Staff → set a person to owner, manager or staff.
- Owner: everything, including staff and the database update.
- Manager: shop settings, payments, offers, coupons and pages. Not staff accounts.
- Staff: orders, products, stock, messages. Not payments or settings.
The first accounts stay owners. You cannot remove your own owner role.

What this panel does not do
It does not take UPI money automatically. It does not email a QR image. It does not replace your accountant, your GST decision, or the delivery PIN list.

Invoices, tray size, seasonal discount, farmer rates, overgrown trays

Open Settings → Invoices for the switches. Open Products → Price lists for the two rate cards. Open a customer and tick Farmer rates if that person should see farmer prices. Customers cannot tick this themselves.

How to turn the seasonal discount on
Settings → Invoices → tick Seasonal discount on → Save. It stays hidden while the box is empty or the dates are outside today (Asia/Kolkata).

How to download an invoice
On a paid order, use Invoice PDF or Print invoice. Print is the one that can show Marathi. Before payment, the desk can open a proforma. It says it is not a tax invoice. A credit note is created when you record a refund on an order that already has an invoice.

Price lists
The PDF is Seasonal (2026-27). The Marathi photo is Farmer. They are not merged. Import is preview, then confirm. New rows stay drafts. The source files were not in the build, so the seasonal CSV is only a header and the farmer CSV is an unverified short list. Check PRICE_LIST_SOURCE.txt before you import.

Bank lines from the PDF were not readable here. Paste the account name and number under shop settings when you have the sheet. Health will stay red until then.

Rollback
database/upgrade.sql only adds columns and tables. It does not delete orders. To roll back, restore the database backup you took before running it. Do not drop the new tables if invoices have already been issued.


Phone numbers and WhatsApp
1. Desk → Phones. The three numbers are 8080370152, 8421577787 and 8421577725.
2. Each number can be turned off, hidden from the site, or removed from WhatsApp.
3. WhatsApp mode: Random keeps one number for that visitor's visit. Round robin moves to the next number for the next visitor. Fixed uses the id you type.
4. If no number is marked for WhatsApp, the WhatsApp buttons disappear.

Rates, varieties and farmer prices
1. Desk → Price lists → Load the supplied sheets. Existing plants keep their photos, stock and descriptions. Their tray price becomes the sheet rate times 150 seedlings.
2. A plant with more than one price is one shop card. The other prices are varieties on that card, not extra cards.
3. New plants start with no stock and a placeholder picture. The dashboard card "Needs photo and description" counts them.
4. Ptilotus is not on the sheet. Leave it as it is.
5. A customer sees farmer varieties only after you open their account and tick Farmer rates.
6. Farmer rates are hidden from the normal shop, search and Google. Approved farmers and staff open Farmer rates.

Bank accounts
1. Desk → Banks. The three accounts from the PDF are loaded as drafts.
2. Check every digit with the bank or a cheque, then tick "I verified these bank details".
3. Tick Show only after that. Until then customers do not see the account number.
4. UPI is unchanged.

Seasonal price list
Desk → Phones has the switch for the public seasonal price list page.
