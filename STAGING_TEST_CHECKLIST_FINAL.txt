3. Desk → Price lists → Load the supplied sheets. Open the shop. You should see new plants such as Alyssum, and you should not see a separate card for every variety.
4. Open Marigold. It should say ₹4.25 per seedling and ₹637.50 per tray (150 seedlings). The variety table should include Vanilla and Hot Pack at their own prices.
5. Ptilotus should still be ₹450.
6. Sign out. Open /farmer-prices. It should say page not found.
7. Mark one customer as Farmer, sign in as them, and open Farmer rates. Marathi and English names should both show. The 50 paise note should not.
8. Footer and contact should show three phone numbers, not the nursery numbers. WhatsApp should stay on the same number while you browse.
9. Desk → Banks. Accounts should be listed and not visible on checkout until you tick verified and show.
10. Place a small order only after you are happy with the new tray prices. Old prices were replaced for the 19 plants that are on the sheet.
