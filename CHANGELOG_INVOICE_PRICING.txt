CHANGELOG — invoices, two price lists, seasonal discount, overgrown trays

PDF engine
includes/pdf.php is a small PDF 1.4 writer written for this shop. It is not FPDF and it has no third-party licence to track. It uses the built-in Helvetica font, prints "Rs", and replaces any non-English letter with a question mark so Marathi is never garbled. The print invoice page is the Unicode copy. No remote font, QR image or API is used.

Rounding
Tray rupees = (per-seedling paise × seedlings + 50) ÷ 100, integer division. Half a rupee rounds up. 4.25 × 150 = 638. 3.75 × 150 = 563. 3.25 × 150 = 488. 4.75 × 150 = 713. Orders stay in whole rupees. Amount in words is whole rupees only (the shop does not store paise on an order).

What did not change
