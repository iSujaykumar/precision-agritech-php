PRECISION AGRITECH — RESULT REPORT
Date: 12 October 2026
Build: homepage + nursery desk (precision-agritech-final-v3)

WHAT CHANGED
- Order calculator is in the top menu (between Price list and Sign in), in the mobile menu, and in the phone bottom bar. It opens on any page. Close with X, Escape, or a tap outside. Focus stays in the panel and returns to the button that opened it. The full page is /calculator. The homepage only has a small button on the hero, not the big form.
- Browse all plants is one carousel with category chips (All, then each category). Arrows on desktop, swipe on a phone, 4 cards on a wide screen, 2 cards at 360px. No-JavaScript visitors still get a sideways row. Full price list and both PDF buttons stay.
- Offers always starts with the real bulk-discount card when the discount is on, then real offer plants. If there are no plant offers it says "More offers soon". No made-up prices.
- Desk > Offers can tick many plants, preview a percent (1 to 50) or a flat seedling price, then create. End this offer now and Undo last bulk offer put the regular price back. Offer prices use offer_seedling_paise and do not overwrite the normal rate. The bulk discount applies on those offer prices unless Settings turns that off. Prices never go under the nursery minimum.
- Plant this month is small cards in a row.
- Homepage section order and on/off are in Desk > Homepage. Default order: Hero, trust, banner, Best sellers, New arrivals, Offers, Plant this month, Browse all plants, Why, Bulk quote band, Reviews, Ready to grow.
- One stock page: /admin/stock. /admin/inventory goes to it. History stays at /admin/inventory-history.
- Desk menu: Today, Orders, Plants, Prices & Offers, Customers, Messages, Homepage, Settings, Help. Settings shows Shop, Phones, Banks and UPI, Delivery, Staff, then More.
- Today has at most 5 "Do this now" buttons, then Orders, Stock and Shop (12 cards). Each number uses the same count as the list it opens. Low and out of stock count shop cards only. GST and PIN tips show once a day and can be hidden. The update banner shows only when the update has not run. Last 7 days sits under the cards.
- Hide, not Delete, on plants, offers, price-list rows, coupons and reviews, with Restore.
- A bug in this pass: the calculator script ran before the panel was on the page, so the menu button did nothing. The panel is now printed first. Plants that share a price can each be picked. Tab stays inside the open panel.
