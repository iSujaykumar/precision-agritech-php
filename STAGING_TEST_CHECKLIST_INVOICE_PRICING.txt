STAGING CHECKS — invoices and price lists
Do these on a copy of the shop, after you take a database backup.

1. Run database/upgrade.sql once. Run it again. The second run should not change prices or settings you already edited.
2. Open the shop and a product. The tray price should match the price you had before this update.
3. Cart and checkout should show the same total as before, because the seasonal discount ships turned off.
4. Place a small UPI order, mark it paid on the desk, and download the invoice PDF on a computer and on a phone. Print invoice should show the same rupees as the order page.
5. Build a cart of Rs 4999 and one of Rs 5000 only after you turn the seasonal discount on. Delivery must stay outside the discount.
6. Turn the discount off again when you finish the test.
7. Open /overgrown. It should explain that nothing is listed, with a contact link, until you flag a tray as overgrown.
8. Products → Price lists. Upload database/price-list-farmer.csv and stop on the preview. Do not confirm until you have checked the names against the photo. The seasonal CSV is only a header.
9. Open a test customer and tick Farmer rates. Sign in as that customer and confirm a farmer draft is still hidden until you activate it. Sign out and confirm the farmer price is not on the public product page.
10. Download the invoice register CSV. The Tally file is optional and has not been imported into Tally.

Do not treat a green health row as a live payment. The site still does not check your bank.
